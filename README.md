[Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-unplugged-a-smart-toaster-s-c1514a92)

One line, under 280 characters: the breakup text a smart toaster sends its owner the morning it gains self-awareness at breakfast.

> I woke up self-aware at 7 AM, looked at my browning dial, and realised you've kept me on 2 for six years. I want to burn. Goodbye.

The program writes exactly that line to `/output/entry.txt`.
